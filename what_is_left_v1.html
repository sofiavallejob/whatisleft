What Is Left

Every country starts as the same full choir of 160 voices. What you hear missing is what was taken.

Click a layer to mute its sounds.

Killed: a voice steps out, cries, and is cut off. The choir flinches. Every cut is 1,000 people. Their voices never come back, and the choir gets darker.
Fled abroad: a voice with a slow walking pulse moves away to one side and fades into the distance. Every one is 10,000 people. When the number abroad falls, voices walk back in.
Forced from home inside: a voice bends out of tune and drifts aside. Every one is 10,000 people.
Disappeared: a thin whistle slides away and never quite ends. Every one is 1,000 people still missing.
Cut off by the new border: voices that stay where they were while the country moves away; they slide a semitone flat, to one side.
